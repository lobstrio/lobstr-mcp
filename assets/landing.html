Three steps to a data‑savvy agent

No SDK, no glue code. Add one URL, sign in with lobstr.io, and start asking.

1

Add the server

Drop the lobstr.io URL into your client's connectors — in Claude, Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.

https://mcp.lobstr.io/mcp
2

Sign in with lobstr.io

A quick browser approval on lobstr.io. You choose what the agent may do — reading is free, running spends credits.

✓ approve · no token to copy

Just ask

Tell your agent what data you want. It finds the right scraper, runs it, and hands back the results.

The toolbox

Everything your agent can do

Fifteen tools, grouped by what they touch. Reading is always safe — running a scraper estimates the cost and asks you first, so nothing spends credits by surprise.

Discover scrapers

Read

Search lobstr.io's catalog, read a scraper's inputs, output fields and pricing, and list the ones you've already saved.

search_scrapers get_scraper_details list_my_scrapers get_my_scraper

Track runs

Read

Check the status and progress of any run — and list the recent runs for a scraper.

get_run list_runs

Read results

Read

Pull a page of scraped results keeping just the fields you need — or grab a link to the full dataset.

get_results get_results_url

Your account

Read

Check your lobstr.io credit balance and see who you're signed in as.

Connected accounts

Read

See the platform accounts (LinkedIn, Facebook, …) your scrapers log in with, and their status — metadata only, never credentials.

Run & manage

Spends credits

Launch a scraper on your behalf — it estimates the cost and waits for your OK first. Stop a run, or clear a scraper's tasks to reuse it (those are free).

run_scraper abort_run empty_scraper
